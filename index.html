Who I am

I grew up inside a family that builds things — companies spanning property services in the US and hospitality in Bangladesh. That upbringing taught me how real businesses actually work long before I ever studied them formally. A degree in Finance gave me the framework; the family enterprise gave me the proving ground.

For over six years, I've grown alongside APX Group — and I've earned every step of the way. I started at the ground level, learning the business from the inside out, and worked my way into the Director role through results, not title. Today I lead strategic operations across our real estate, property preservation, hospitality, and technology ventures — but my real value isn't just keeping things running, it's pushing them forward.

My edge is that I don't just oversee — I build. Financial dashboards, QC frameworks, AI-powered tools, and automation pipelines that make every operation run sharper. I believe deeply in the power of technology to change lives — not just in business, but as a genuine force for good in communities that need it most.

Where AI Meets Operations

I don't just use AI. I build operations with it. The tools will keep changing — staying current isn't a hobby, it's the job.

Claude, Cursor, Vercel, Supabase, Perplexity, n8n — I work across the stack, because the edge was never a single tool. It's knowing how to wire them together into something a business actually runs on.

AI Agents

Designing autonomous agents that handle multi-step work end to end — with human approval checkpoints built in where judgment matters.

Workflow Automation

Connecting apps, inboxes, and triggers into reliable pipelines so operations run themselves instead of someone's to-do list.

Vision & Multimodal

Putting AI to work on images and documents, not just text — reading, categorizing, extracting, and generating from visual inputs.

AI-Assisted Development

Shipping real applications with AI as a co-builder — from scoping and architecture to a working front-to-back product.

Decision Intelligence

Turning messy operational data into dashboards, KPIs, and clear signals — with AI doing the heavy synthesis.

Integrations & Tooling

Wiring AI into the systems a business already runs on — building custom connectors and reusable workflows, not one-off prompts.

I'm betting on AI early because the trajectory is obvious: it isn't a feature, it's the next operating layer for entire businesses. In Bangladesh — where AI adoption in traditional industry is near zero — being early isn't a risk, it's the advantage.
